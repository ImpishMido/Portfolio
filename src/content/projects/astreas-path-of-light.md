## What it is

A 3D metroidvania where the player controls Astrea on her quest to defeat evil.
Made for our school's game jam.

## What I made

As the sole Sprite Artist I was responsible for bringing our Character Designers' concepts to digital and animating them.
I also created the Title Art, Buttons, animated the UIs and designed the icons.

## What I learned

Working based off a team member's work and practicing with puppet animation.
